Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Runagotchi is a small, non-commercial app: a virtual pet that is fed by the kilometres you run. This page explains which personal data the app processes, why, and what rights you have under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What data we process

DataWhat it is
AccountYour email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our login provider; nobody can read it.
ProfileYour username.
PetYour pet's name, look, appetite setting, hatch date and current state (food, level).
RunsFor each run: date and time, distance, duration (if known) and where it came from (entered by hand, Garmin, COROS or Suunto).
FriendsFriend requests and friendships between usernames.
ChallengesChallenges you create or join (distance, time limit, participants), and kudos or encouragement you send and receive.
Watch connectionsIf you connect a Garmin, COROS or Suunto account: your user ID at that brand and the access keys it issues, so new runs can be received. From the activities the brand sends, we keep only running activities (start time, distance, duration and the brand's activity ID); other sports are discarded.
Technical dataWhen you use the app, our hosting providers process your IP address and basic request information in server logs for security and operation.
Data on your deviceYour browser stores your login session (so you stay logged in) and, if you use the app without an account, your pet and runs. This is technically necessary; there are no tracking or advertising cookies.

3. Why, and on what legal basis

  • Providing the app (account, pet, runs, friends, challenges, watch connections): to perform the service you signed up for, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR. Connecting a watch is always your own choice and can be undone at any time.
  • Security and operation (server logs): our legitimate interest in running the app safely, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Runagotchi has no advertising, no analytics or tracking, does not sell data, and does not use your data to train AI models.

4. Who can see your data

  • Your friends (people whose friend request you accepted, or who accepted yours) see your username and your pet: its name, look, hunger and level. They never see your individual runs.
  • People in a challenge with you see your username, the total distance you ran during that challenge, and kudos or encouragement.
  • Anyone logged in can look up whether a username exists, in order to send a friend request.

5. Service providers

We use these providers, who process data only on our behalf under data processing agreements:

  • Supabase Inc. (USA) – database, login and server functions. Our data is stored in their EU region (Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Netlify Inc. (USA) – hosting of the web app, including server logs.

Where data may be accessed from outside the EU, the transfer is protected by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and, where the provider is certified, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Garmin, COROS and Suunto are only involved if you connect one of their accounts. They are separate controllers of the data in their own services; their own privacy policies apply there. With your permission they send us your new activities, as described in section 2.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account, pet, runs, friends and challenges: as long as your account exists. Runs you delete in the app are deleted immediately.
  • Watch access keys: until you disconnect the watch, which deletes them immediately. Runs already imported stay in your log until you delete them.
  • To delete your whole account and all its data, email us. We will do so within 30 days.
  • Server logs: for the short periods set by our hosting providers.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict its processing, to data portability, and to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 15–21 GDPR). Email us to use any of these rights.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Austria this is the Österreichische Datenschutzbehörde, Barichgasse 40–42, 1030 Wien, www.dsb.gv.at.

8. Changes

If the app changes in a way that affects your data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
